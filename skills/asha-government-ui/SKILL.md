# Apply the ASHA government UI

Read [design system](../../docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) and the relevant screen in [UI/UX](../../docs/UI_UX_SPEC.md). Use [design tokens](../../design/tokens.json) as the machine-readable palette and spacing reference.

Use utility bar, original identity header, navy navigation, restrained saffron decoration, structured panels and source/help footer. Keep ASHA task screens compact and usable on a phone. Do not introduce a marketing hero, decorative AI chat area or copied government logo into operational pages.

Check the visible prototype label, Hindi wrapping, keyboard focus, status words/icons, inline errors, offline/stale indicators and 320-pixel reflow. A successful desktop screenshot does not verify mobile or access states.

The portal captures in [design research](../../docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md) are references, not assets licensed for copying into our branding. Review any colour pairing against the actual foreground/background, especially saffron.
