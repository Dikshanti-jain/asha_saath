# ASHA + NGO research pack

Research cut-off: 8 October 2026, India.

Start with **asha-ngo-decision-brief.md** for the quick Hinglish explanation and recommended demo. **asha-ngo-full-report.md** is the editable complete report. The PDF in **output/pdf/** is the linked reading version, with navigation and readable entries for long comparison rows.

The catalogue contains **24 additional schemes/programmes and 30 platforms/tools**. ASHA and the SSBSK operational framework are discussed separately. Related programmes and systems overlap; these counts are not unique beneficiaries, companies or market estimates. Overseas comparators and Shaishav's described-versus-verified rollout status are labelled.

Ten distinct research-agent notes are numbered 01 through 10 in **research/**. The parent also wrote the research method, operating model and additional comparator review. Scheme eligibility, local services and capacity must be confirmed with the responsible authority or provider before use.

The recommended MVP uses 20-30 visibly synthetic visit tasks, one document-navigation service and three fictional provider offers. It demonstrates the original visit-planning requirements and one complete support journey. It does not imply government access, actual participating NGOs, money raised or clinical outcomes.

The full report includes source dates, comparison matrices, stakeholder perspectives, network breakpoints, referral/funding states, privacy and offline design, MVP acceptance criteria, a pitch and an independent audit. Source and provider claims are distinguished from measured field evidence and proposed features.

No live platform was built or deployed as part of this research request. No patients, NGOs, funders or officials were contacted. The proposed live pilot still needs current approved rules, willing providers with real capacity, funded coordination and approved data-handling arrangements.
