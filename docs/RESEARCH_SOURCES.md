## 3. Evidence rules for future work

Refresh exact clinical schedules, eligibility, vendor terms and package APIs from current primary sources before use. Record jurisdiction/version/effective date/local approval. A website footer update is not proof every rule was revised.

No field study, ASHA interview, market adoption estimate, live provider capacity, funding commitment, measured health outcome or winning probability is established by this pack.
