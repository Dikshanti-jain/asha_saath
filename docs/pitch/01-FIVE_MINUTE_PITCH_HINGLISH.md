# ASHA Saath — five-minute conversational pitch

Revised and fact-checked 9 October 2026. Read the speech paragraphs only. The opening is an illustrative scenario, not a field interview. Aim for 125–135 words/minute with three short screen cues; time one full rehearsal.

## 0:00–0:40 — start with a situation

[Look at the judges. Speak as if explaining the app to one mentor.]

<!-- speech -->
Namaste. Sochiye, ek ASHA ke paas aaj ke ghar-ghar dauron ki list hai. Kuch ghar paas hain, kuch door. Ek visit ki date nikal rahi hai. Din ka time limited hai.

Ab use do decisions lene hain: pehle kahan jaana hai, aur jo kaam aaj nahi ho paayega, uska kya karna hai?

Daure ke beech ek family kehti hai, “Scheme ke liye apply karna hai, lekin documents samajh nahi aa rahe.”

Yahin se hamara project shuru hota hai: **ASHA Saath.**
<!-- /speech -->

## 0:40–1:10 — give scale and a clear purpose

<!-- speech -->
Government ke December 2025 ke data ke hisaab se desh mein dus lakh chaalis hazaar se zyada ASHAs thi.

Hamne is problem ko do connected parts mein dekha: ASHA ka din theek se plan ho, aur visit mein jo madad maangi gayi, uska follow-up beech mein na chhoote.
<!-- /speech -->

Source note, not spoken: [PIB, 20 September 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2312619&lang=1&reg=48), count as of **31 December 2025**, over **10.40 lakh**. Reopened and verified 9 October 2026. The number establishes scale; it does not measure workload or prove our proposed gap.

## 1:10–2:05 — show the plan

[Point to the daily plan and one unserved reason. Keep clicking to a minimum.]

<!-- speech -->
Pehle planning dekhiye. Coordinator visit list upload karta hai. Usmein priority aur due date pehle se approved hoti hain. Hamara system available time aur travel ka estimate dekhkar din ki visit list banata hai.

Sirf paas wale ghar chun lena achhi planning nahi hai. Kisi door ke visit ki priority zyada hai, toh uski importance distance se kam nahi honi chahiye.

Jo visit fit nahi hota, woh list se gayab nahi hota. Uska reason dikhta hai. Coordinator dekh sakta hai ki extra time, doosre din ki planning, ya health team ki action kahan chahiye.

ASHA ke phone par agla visit aur uska result record karne ka seedha button hai. Map se visit ka order bhi samajh aata hai.
<!-- /speech -->

## 2:05–3:25 — return to the same family

[Point to the help request: selected NGO → permission → next action.]

<!-- speech -->
Ab usi family par wapas aate hain jise documents mein help chahiye.

ASHA ek chhota request record karti hai. Uske baad NGOs ko follow-up karne ki responsibility coordinator ki hai. Hum ASHA ke upar ek aur chasing ka kaam nahi daalna chahte.

Sirf NGO ka naam kaafi nahi hai. Kya woh yeh help deta hai, is area mein kaam karta hai, aur abhi capacity hai? System suitable offer dikhata hai.

Person ko exact NGO aur share hone wali information bataakar permission lete hain. NGO accept kare, toh slot reserve hota hai.

NGO ke “help delivered” bolne se request close nahi hoti. Person se alag confirmation ke baad coordinator close karta hai.

Agar service centre jaane ke liye paanch sau rupaye ki help chahiye, uska separate request aur permission hai. NGO approve kare toh budget reserve hota hai. Payment report ke baad person se amount confirm karte hain. Amount alag ho, toh dispute dikhta hai. Isi case mein ASHA, coordinator aur NGO message bhi kar sakte hain.
<!-- /speech -->

## 3:25–4:10 — say what is actually ready

<!-- speech -->
Aaj yeh prototype 24 fictional tasks, do ASHA accounts aur teen fictional offers par working hai. Document guidance, cash-assistance records aur case messages database mein save hote hain. Real NGOs onboard nahi hue; demo cash flow money transfer nahi karta.

Government scheme ki eligibility aur approval uski authority ke paas hi rehte hain. Urgent health concern aaye, toh uski alag health-team process hai.

Existing visit aur referral tools hain. Hamara focus is poore safar ko jodna hai: visit decide hone se lekar family ko help milne ki confirmation tak.
<!-- /speech -->

## 4:10–5:00 — practical benefit and ask

<!-- speech -->
ASHA ko agla kaam clear. Family ko pata ki request kahan atki hai. NGO ko apni capacity ke hisaab se kaam. Coordinator ko pata ki kis request par action lena hai.

Next step: ek PHC area, do ASHAs, funded coordinator aur do-teen local NGOs. Service aur area verify karke actual requests par pilot karenge.

Hum dekhenge: due visits kitne complete hue, help kitne time mein mili, aur staff ka extra kaam kitna badha ya ghata.

Humein ab field partners aur feedback chahiye. **Hamari koshish hai ki “madad ke liye bhej diya” ke baad bhi us person ka kaam kisi ki responsibility bana rahe.**

Thank you.
<!-- /speech -->

## Rehearsal notes — do not read aloud

- Memorise six beats: **morning list → scale → plan → same family → confirmation → pilot**. Exact words may change; facts and feature boundaries must not.
- Read once, then explain from the [cue card](05-DELIVERY_AND_CLAIM_REVIEW.md). Avoid reciting headings, repeated slogans, English abstractions and every feature name.
- Three brief screen cues are enough. Choose the document or cash/message flow for a separate role-switching demonstration; showing both takes longer.
- Say “dus lakh chaalis hazaar se zyada” and retain “December 2025”. Do not imply an October workforce count.
- NFHS-6 **65.2%** remains verified supporting context in [evidence notes](02-EVIDENCE_AND_BENEFITS.md), not a spoken proof that our app fixes maternal-health outcomes. Moving it out makes the speech clearer without losing the source.
- Software evidence for Q&A: **31/31 Vitest + 15/15 HTTP/SQLite tests**, plus **8/8 independent HTTP/SQLite audit checks** passed. These are separate software checks, not field trials. No travel saving, real partnership or health-impact claim.
- [Current NGO-support review](../NGO_SUPPORT_REVIEW_2026-10-09.md) records the cash/message checks; the [UI trust review](../UI_TRUST_REVIEW_2026-10-09.md) records the preceding visual revision. Demo accounts are open fictional roles, maps show fictional visit order, and real payments/offline integrations remain future scope.
