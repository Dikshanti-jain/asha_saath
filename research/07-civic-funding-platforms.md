# Eight India civic-benefit and funding services for an ASHA visit optimizer

Research cut-off and access date: **8 October 2026**. This is an assessment of official public documentation and operator claims; no accounts were created, applications submitted, donations collected, or outreach performed. Dated releases establish historical facts; current pages establish advertised capabilities, not independently measured service quality. The handoff column below is a **proposed operational use**, not a claim that each platform already implements a shared ASHA-to-district workflow.

Exactly **eight service rows** are reserved here. National CSR Exchange and National CSR data reporting have different functions but belong to the same Ministry of Corporate Affairs ecosystem. They are not two independent companies or independent funding markets. The historical National CSR Portal and current CDM CSR-data access are counted once in row 5.

## Current and dated evidence, with detail URLs

1. **myScheme.** PIB records dedication of the discovery platform on **4 July 2022**: [Digital India Week release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1839175&lang=2&reg=48). The [current official FAQ](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/faqs) bears **18 September 2026, v3.1.27**. It distinguishes 15-language content from a Hindi/English voice/text chatbot, and acknowledges continued scheme onboarding. A successful screening answer is provisional evidence for a department application, not a sanctioned benefit.

2. **UMANG.** The [official eBook](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/flipbook/index.html) dates dedication to **23 November 2017**. The [current homepage](https://web.umang.gov.in/) bears **15 January 2026** and provides support hours; its machine-readable statistics displayed zero placeholders, so those counters were not treated as actual totals. A [PIB June 2026 account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=158744&lang=2&reg=3) reports 11.39 crore registrations as of **2 June 2026**. The [government eBook](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/flipbook/index.html) describes RCH ANC/immunization calendar access, but this historical description alone does not verify a live ASHA scheduling or household-routing feature.

3. **eMitra.** The [tariff page](https://emitra.rajasthan.gov.in/emitra/rate), indexed with **26 February 2026** update date, says the listed service rates include taxes, while departmental charges are extra. It offers an overcharging complaint route. Confirm service-specific exceptions and the amount shown on the receipt. The [Rajasthan department page](https://sectors.rajasthan.gov.in/pages/department-page/1441) discusses scheme enrolment and rate-setting; some scheme names in that descriptive page may be historical and must be checked against the live service catalogue.

4. **CSR Exchange.** A [6 June 2022 MCA/PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1831559&lang=2&reg=48) announces the next day's launch. A [Lok Sabha answer dated 18 December 2023](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/1714/AU2391.pdf?source=pqals) confirms launch in June 2022 and, **as of 7 December 2023**, 838 companies, 6,123 agencies, 34 RFPs and 435 RFFs. These are dated participation counts, not 2026 totals or evidence of completed grants. The same answer documents login/registration/data queries and a helpdesk response; it does not support alleging universal platform failure.

5. **CSR data service and counting.** The [9 February 2018 MCA/PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1520212&lang=2&reg=48) identifies csr.gov.in as a disclosure-based National CSR Data Portal. The [current CDM CSR-data view](https://mcacdm.nic.in/csr-data) supplies present public access to CSR reports. This report uses one historical/current data-service row, rather than inventing a separate additional competitor for CDM. The [CDM FAQ](https://mcacdm.nic.in/faq) identifies MCA21 forms/attachments as report inputs; the [CDM about page](https://mcacdm.nic.in/about-us) explains the analytics/data-warehouse role. The precise transition date from the legacy site was not established. Snapshot date and reporting fiscal year must both be shown.

6. **Haqdarshak.** The operator-linked [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.haqdarshak.jana&hl=en_IN) was updated **28 July 2026**, lists 6,000+ schemes and 10+ languages, and characterizes the app as information/guidance rather than government processing or approval. The [operator website](https://www.haqdarshak.com/) instead reports 5,000+ programmes and more than 14 local languages across its broader offer. Record these as different current operator descriptions, not one reconciled audited number. The website's field/API offer and app's narrower disclaimer can coexist; a partnership still needs scope and coverage verification. No public rate card, endpoint documentation or district service-level commitment was established.

7. **Milaap.** The [operator overview](https://milaap.org/about-us/overview) dates founding to **2010**. The [official pricing page](https://www.milaap.org/crowdfunding/pricing) publishes gateway ranges effective **1 April 2026**: **0.6–1.5% for INR** and **0.25–2.8% for USD**. Its 0% platform-fee statement applies to individuals/NGOs/organizations; selected outreach is subject to case assessment. The [terms](https://milaap.org/about-us/terms-and-conditions) contain generic platform-fee language and expressly allow holds, withdrawal approval and outreach costs. Therefore retain the current campaign's actual written fee arrangement; do not equate the headline with guaranteed zero deductions or a payout deadline. An [operator NGO article dated 13 July 2024](https://pages.milaap.org/2024/07/13/milaap-for-ngos-how-non-profit-organizations-can-leverage-crowdfunding-for-impact/) describes partial withdrawals and cause/expense verification; these are conditional processes.

8. **Ketto.** The [operator overview](https://www.ketto.org/blog/ketto/) dates founding to **2012**. The current [plan page](https://www.ketto.org/support/plans-and-pricing.php) lists **18% GST on the success/platform and gateway fees**, rather than on the entire amount raised. It reserves pricing changes and mentions possible advertising and other campaign expenses. Exact total deductions therefore need campaign-specific confirmation. [Withdrawal help](https://customerhappiness.ketto.org/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-i-withdraw-the-funds-i-have-raised-7-8-2021) requires approved documents and cleared funds; its indicated working-day timelines are conditional guidance, not universal emergency guarantees. The [terms](https://www.ketto.org/terms-of-use.php) also require permission for depicted people and parents/guardians for minors, with informed explanation of wide image use.

## Design implications for the ASHA + local NGO assistance layer

These are **design hypotheses to test locally**, not findings that every existing service fails:

- Separate the beneficiary's record into scheme candidates, applications, official decisions, NGO service commitments, corporate proposals, donations received and verified expenditure. A discovery match, CSR expression of interest and published fundraiser represent different stages.
- Give each referral an owner, receiving organization/contact, requested action, permission record, acknowledgement, expected next update and escalation route. The public sources reviewed do not establish one shared district referral contract across these eight services.
- Start with linked official pages and receipt-based reconciliation. Pursue a Haqdarshak commercial API or government integration only after scope, access rights, security responsibilities and permitted data exchange are agreed; avoid inferring access from an app's internal APIs.
- For Rajasthan, make kiosk charges visible and distinguish service fees from departmental fees. Elsewhere, identify the appropriate local assisted-service channel rather than importing Rajasthan eMitra as a national network.
- Keep a district NGO directory independently checked for service category, operating area, present capacity and eligibility. CSR portal registration, campaign presence and historical spending are insufficient evidence of an NGO's ability to accept a particular referral today.
- Use aggregate, documented unmet needs for possible CSR projects. The government release and Exchange FAQ describe company-board control; neither creates a pooled cash balance that the optimizer can allocate. Crowdfunding may supplement a documented residual need but donor response and net payout remain uncertain.
- Clinical urgency and ASHA visit priorities need their own authorized health workflow. None of the reviewed documentation established an end-to-end clinical triage, route optimization, NGO acceptance and funding approval engine. This is a bounded evidence gap to validate through product demonstrations and a district pilot, not proof that such private integrations do not exist.

## Key corrections for synthesis

**myScheme ≠ sanction; UMANG availability ≠ department approval; Rajasthan eMitra ≠ nationwide/free assisted delivery; CSR disclosures ≠ available grants; CSR Exchange ≠ pooled cash or guaranteed funding; Haqdarshak ≠ government authority and is more than a discovery-only website; 0% crowdfunding platform fee ≠ zero total deductions; published crowdfunding campaign ≠ donations or immediate payout.**

No claim of an open API should be made for six services where public documentation was not verified. For **UMANG**, distinguish official integration architecture from access authorization. For **Haqdarshak**, distinguish an operator's commercial SaaS/API offer from published endpoint documentation. Across all eight, absence of public verification is a research limit, not evidence that no private integration exists.
