# Eight NGO discovery, funding and volunteering platforms

Research cut-off and retrieval date: **8 October 2026**. Scope: exactly eight platform ecosystems. Give.do, Give Discover and Give Grants are counted together as **Give**. This is public desk research using operator/government sources; no accounts, paid tools, API calls, outreach or referral tests were undertaken. “Documented” means provider-described, not independently demonstrated performance.

These platforms can help an ASHA coordinator identify organizations and mobilize resources. Their public evidence does not establish a ready-made district-level ASHA referral service with accepted cases, current village catchments and confirmed spare capacity. That is an **integration and field-validation question**, not proof that every operator lacks such capabilities.

## What this means for an ASHA visit optimizer

**Proposed pilot approach:** use Darpan for identity cross-checking, GuideStar India and Give Discover for a shortlist, and direct partner onboarding to establish service availability. Approach iVolunteer/ConnectFor for suitable volunteer support and Goodera for scheduled corporate participation or in-kind work. Treat Give, GlobalGiving and relevant NGOBOX calls as longer-term program/funding channels, with eligible nonprofit partners and explicit budgets.

Keep organizational discovery separate from operational matching. An organization's registered office, pincode, district label or national presence claim is not its actual beneficiary catchment. A directory total is not the number of active organizations available to support one village this week. GuideStar India, iVolunteer and Give pages have multiple historical counts; this report deliberately avoids turning them into an addressable active-service estimate.

**Pilot directory fields to validate with each partner:** legal name/identifiers; evidence source and certification expiry; service category; covered villages/blocks; referral eligibility; exclusions; current staff/stock/budget; response window; contact owner; accessibility/languages; referral route; acceptance/decline reason; next availability date; and completion feedback. These are proposed product requirements, not features claimed to exist on every platform.

An ASHA-recorded request for food, accompaniment, disability support or entitlement assistance should reach a partner only after a coordinator checks suitability and the resident consents to necessary sharing. Aggregated needs can inform a donor proposal without disclosing patient names publicly. Clinical urgency must continue through the appropriate health-system pathway; a fundraising or volunteering search is not an emergency response mechanism.

## Evidence distinctions and corrections for synthesis

- **Darpan identity is not endorsement.** The portal's notice expressly denies endorsement, affiliation and recognition; do not label an NGO “NITI approved.” Its signup guide describes document checks for ID generation, which should not be translated into impact certification. [Notice](https://ngodarpan.gov.in/?lang=de), [official signup guide](https://ngodarpan.gov.in/assets/ngo_doc/NGO_Darpan_How_to_Signup_English.pdf).
- **Verification differs by level and content.** GuideStar certification is separate from its explicitly unvetted user announcements. Give transparency stars describe profile completeness, while diligence seals describe checks. A historical badge or listing must be checked for current validity. [GuideStar](https://www.guidestarindia.org/), [Give standards](https://discover.give.do/news/give-launches-guaranteed-trust-seals/).
- **Funding is conditional.** Access to donors, grant listings, CSR buyers or a fundraising account does not guarantee funds, unrestricted spending, medical reimbursement or fast disbursement. GlobalGiving does document unrestricted fundraising as an option; do not overcorrect by claiming all platform funding is restricted. [GlobalGiving explanation](https://www.globalgiving.org/learn/how-globalgiving-vets-nonprofits/).
- **Two APIs are documented; one is announced as future.** GlobalGiving and Goodera have official API documentation. GuideStar India's homepage says “Coming soon.” API access for the remaining five was not verified, rather than proven absent. No authenticated functionality, pricing, SLA or production access was tested.
- **Operators must be named accurately.** Current Give platform terms identify SaathiRe; NGOBOX's current footer identifies Renalysis Consultants; GuideStar India is CSISI; iVolunteer is MITRA's program; ConnectFor is Rosy Blue Foundation's program. Historical GiveIndia legal documents should not override current platform terms.
- **Capacity and workflow gaps remain hypotheses.** None of the reviewed public pages proves a ready live directory of rural health-support capacity integrated with ASHA schedules. This supports testing a coordinator-managed referral layer; it does not establish that existing platforms cannot add or already privately provide one.

## Source provenance and practical limits

All substantive factual sources above are government or platform-operator pages. NITI's VAC page establishes Darpan's current institutional role. Direct Darpan page extraction returned no body text; the official portal's indexed notice and official signup PDF supplied the registration/endorsement evidence. NITI's directory-use article is older context: its historical NGO count was not reused as a 2026 total.

GuideStar's current homepage supplies operator identity, search, certification categories, user-content warning and the future API label. Its resource site distinguishes active certification lists. Give's January 2026 hosted platform terms supply current operator identity; Discover, Give.do overview, seal criteria and Give Grants diligence page establish distinct discovery, fundraising and assessment services. Discover shows inconsistent/historical seal counts in different blocks; those counts were excluded.

GlobalGiving's nonprofit terms identify US/UK operators. Its vetting/compliance sources establish recurring review/reporting; its 29 May 2025 operator interview explicitly explains unrestricted fundraising. API terms/documentation establish an integration offering, not acceptance of this proposed use case. [Ongoing compliance](https://www.globalgiving.org/compliance-policies/).

NGOBOX's About page/footer supplies current operator and listing/services evidence. Goodera's nonprofit pages, terms and official API page establish current service and developer offerings; its MCP article is a **provider announcement**, not independently validated readiness or an endorsement of the article's “first” claim. iVolunteer's NGO service and signup pages establish the VMS and onboarding workflow; no unpublished verification methodology was inferred. ConnectFor's current About/NGO/volunteer/corporate pages establish current scope, while its indexed beta onboarding terms are a specifically identified older/legacy contractual source.

Page retrieval or crawler recency is not a feature launch/update date. Public documentation can be incomplete or outdated; contract, access rights, local reach and service availability require operator/partner confirmation before a deployment.
