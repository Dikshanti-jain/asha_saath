# Research method and evidence limits

Research cut-off: 8 October 2026. This work supports Healthcare 03 at Codefiesta 5.0 and the team's proposed ASHA-to-NGO support extension.

The user requested ten research agents. Ten distinct research assignments are being completed in batches because only three child agents can run concurrently. The parent also researches and synthesizes evidence. This is desk research, not stakeholder interviews, authenticated product testing, legal certification, or a field evaluation.

Evidence categories used throughout the report:

- **Official:** government guidelines, scheme pages, parliamentary answers and official review reports. Eligibility, rollout and benefits are date-sensitive.
- **Provider-documented:** product operators' descriptions and documentation. Features and claimed reach are not independently validated outcomes.
- **Study-specific:** published research with a particular geography, sample and period. Findings are not automatically national estimates.
- **Design proposal:** our suggested implementation, targets, architecture or operational model.
- **Validation question:** an unconfirmed operational gap to investigate with practitioners.

Connected Tavily research returned transport timeouts (HTTP 504); searches/extracts also encountered usage limits. Research continued using direct web searches and primary-source pages. No successful automated Tavily deep-research report is being claimed.

The official hackathon page was read earlier in this conversation with expanded statements. The current extraction reconfirmed the Healthcare 03 title but did not expand the requirements. The original expanded requirements are retained with that provenance; current direct web access to the page was also limited.

Counts describe catalogued schemes/programs and software/services, not unique beneficiary populations or unique companies. Related programs may share infrastructure, eligibility or beneficiaries. In particular, UIP and Mission Indradhanush overlap; NTEP and PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan complement each other. A large registration or expenditure total is not a startup's obtainable market, a fund balance or a count of active, available local services.

Geographic coverage, staff capacity, funding availability, API access and willingness to participate must be confirmed with each partner. A feature not described on a public webpage is **undocumented in this review**, not proven absent. No platform is declared universally broken or the proposed system globally unprecedented.

No NGOs, patients, funders or government officials were contacted; no money was requested or transferred; no live health-system access was obtained.
